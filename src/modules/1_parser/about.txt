Module 1: Document Reader & Text Cleaner

    Job: Reads raw .txt files from a folder and turns long strings of text into clean lists of words.

    What it does step-by-step:
        Opens file doc1.txt.
        Converts all letters to lowercase ("Cat" $\rightarrow$ "cat").
        Removes punctuation ("hello!" $\rightarrow$ "hello").
        Filters out useless filler words like "the", "is", "at" (called stop words).

    Output: Clean word tokens sent to Module 2.
